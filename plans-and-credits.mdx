## Understanding credits

Credits power generation. Each still or video draw from your balance. Exact cost depends on the node, model, and settings you choose — **the cost is shown on the generator before you run**.

Broadly:

- Faster / standard image models cost fewer credits
- Higher-fidelity image models and higher resolutions cost more
- Video costs more than a typical still

Your monthly credits renew with your subscription. Unused subscription credits **roll over**. One-time top-ups from **Manage Plan** stay available until you use them.

## Free account vs paid generation

You can create a free account and explore projects, libraries, and workflows. **Generating images or video, and downloading outputs, requires an active paid plan** with credits. Rainfrog does not offer a separate free generation trial — billing starts when you subscribe, and credits are available right away.

## Billing & subscription

- **Monthly** — pay month to month
- **Annual** — pay yearly and save **20%**; credits are still added monthly

Manage upgrades, top-ups, billing portal, pause, and cancel from the **Manage Plan** tab. See [Manage Plan](/account/manage-plan).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do unused credits roll over?">
    Yes. Unused subscription credits roll over when your plan renews. One-time top-up credits stay until you use them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    There is no free generation trial. A free account can explore the studio; paid plans unlock generation and downloads. Cancel anytime from **Manage Plan** if a plan is not right for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    Open **Manage Plan** and select **Cancel subscription**. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Rainfrog does not issue prorated refunds for unused time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I switch plans?">
    Yes. Upgrade or change plans from **Manage Plan** / pricing. After a successful upgrade, the new credit allotment and limits apply for the plan you selected. Downgrades follow your billing change and may reduce limits without deleting existing items.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I pick an image model?">
    Open the Image Generator model picker. Use a faster model while exploring, then switch to a higher-fidelity model for final frames. Credit cost updates on the node as you change settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
