## Understanding credits

Credits are the currency of generation in Rainfrog. Each image or video clip you generate draws from your credit balance. The exact cost per generation depends on which model you use:

- **Normal image models** — standard quality outputs at a lower credit cost per generation
- **Pro image models** — higher-quality, more detailed outputs that cost more credits per generation

Your monthly credits renew automatically when your subscription renews. Any credits you haven't used accumulate — they roll over rather than expire at the end of the billing period. If you need more credits mid-cycle, you can purchase a one-time top-up from the **Manage Plan** tab; top-up credits never expire.

## Billing & subscription

Rainfrog offers two billing cycles:

- **Monthly** — pay month to month with no long-term commitment
- **Annual** — pay for a full year upfront and save **20%** on your monthly rate

Regardless of which cycle you choose, your credits are added monthly on your renewal date — not all at once at the start of the year. This means annual subscribers receive a fresh credit allocation each month throughout their subscription term.

## Getting started

Rainfrog does not offer a free trial. To generate images or videos, you need an active paid plan with a credit balance. Sign up and subscribe at [app.rainfrog.ai/signup](https://app.rainfrog.ai/signup) — billing starts immediately and your credits are available right away. You can choose monthly or annual billing at checkout, and you can cancel or switch plans at any time from the **Manage Plan** tab inside the studio.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do unused credits roll over?">
    Yes. Credits you don't use in a given billing period roll over and accumulate on your account — they don't disappear at renewal. One-time top-up credits work the same way and never expire, regardless of your subscription status.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Rainfrog does not offer a free trial. When you subscribe to a paid plan, billing starts immediately and your credits are available right away. You can cancel at any time from the **Manage Plan** tab if the plan isn't right for you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    Open the **Manage Plan** tab inside Rainfrog and select **Cancel Subscription**. Your cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, so you retain access to your plan and remaining credits until then. Rainfrog does not issue prorated refunds for unused time in a billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I switch plans?">
    Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the **Manage Plan** tab. Switching plans adjusts your credit allowance and feature limits on your next billing cycle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between normal and pro image models?">
    Both model tiers are available on every paid plan. Normal image models generate solid, campaign-ready outputs at a lower credit cost per image — a good choice for high-volume iteration. Pro image models produce higher-quality, more detailed results and are well-suited for final outputs, hero images, and print-ready assets. Pro generations cost more credits per image than normal generations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
