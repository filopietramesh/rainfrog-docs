## How Rainfrog works

Rainfrog replaces prompt engineering with visual composition. You build a node graph — your assets as inputs, a generator node as the output — and Rainfrog assembles the AI instructions automatically behind the scenes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your assets">
    Add your products, characters, style references, and environments to your asset libraries. Upload once and reuse across every campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a workflow">
    Open the canvas in a project, drag your asset nodes onto it, and connect them to a generator node. Or start from a ready-made template in the public gallery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate** and Rainfrog produces a full campaign set — multiple visually consistent images (or video) in one run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Iterate and reuse">
    Swap a product node, change the environment, or adjust the style — then regenerate. Save your workflow as a reusable template for the next campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>
