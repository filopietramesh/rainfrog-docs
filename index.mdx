## How Rainfrog works

Rainfrog replaces prompt engineering with visual composition. You build a node graph — your assets as inputs, generators as outputs — and Rainfrog assembles the generation automatically.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your assets">
    Add products, characters, and environments under **Assets**. Choose styles on the Style node in the studio. Upload once and reuse across campaigns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a workflow">
    Open a project, add nodes from the floating dock, and connect them to an Image Generator (or Full Body, Outfit Sheet, or Video Generator). Or start from a studio template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate**. Rainfrog produces stills or video on the generator node. Previous runs stay in history so you can compare.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Iterate and reuse">
    Swap a product, change the environment, or adjust the style — then regenerate. Clone gallery workflows from **Workflows** when you want a head start.
  </Step>
</Steps>
