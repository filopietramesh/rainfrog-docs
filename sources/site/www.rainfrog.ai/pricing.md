## Billing & credits

How Rainfrog subscriptions, credits, and cancellations work.

Do unused credits roll over?+

Yes. When your subscription renews, Rainfrog adds your full monthly credit allotment to whatever balance you have left—unused credits accumulate. One-time top-up credits never expire and stay in your account until used.

How do billing periods work?+

Rainfrog offers monthly and annual subscriptions on Hobby, Pro, and Ultra. Monthly plans renew every month. Annual plans are billed once per year at a 20% discount versus paying monthly. Every plan includes a monthly credit allotment; on annual billing, credits are added each month on your renewal date.

Is there a free trial?+

Rainfrog is an independent, bootstrapped product. We do not offer free trials on paid plans—subscribing starts billing immediately. You can create a free account to explore the studio and workflows before choosing a plan.

How do I cancel my subscription?+

You can cancel anytime from Manage Plan in the studio. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and you keep access until then. Rainfrog does not offer prorated refunds for unused time or credits.

How can I collaborate or partner with Rainfrog?+

For partnerships, press, integrations, or other collaboration inquiries, email info@rainfrog.ai. For product help, contact support@rainfrog.ai.
