## What Rainfrog Actually Does

Rainfrog replaces the prompt box with a canvas. You connect your own products, characters, styles, and environments as nodes, and the connections between them assemble the prompt automatically in the backend — you never type a word of AI language.

Everything you upload becomes a reusable asset. The platform auto-analyzes and tags products, characters, and environments the moment they're added, so their full context travels with them into every new workflow — no re-uploading the same hero shot for the fifth campaign in a row. Existing workflows can be cloned and remixed instead of rebuilt from a blank canvas, and preset templates like Fashion Shoot, Product Still Life, and UGC Content give you a working starting point for the formats you shoot most.
