## What is Rainfrog AI?

Unlike prompt-based tools, Rainfrog replaces prompt-writing with visual composition — campaign consistency comes from reusable nodes, not from re-typing the same character or style description into every prompt. Users build workflows by connecting nodes on a canvas—products, characters, styles, environments, cameras, and text—into generator nodes that produce campaign-ready images and video.

A graph engine traverses those connections, resolves every node's output, and assembles the underlying AI prompt automatically, with creative taste already built in. The person directing the workflow never has to write or engineer a prompt by hand.

## What it does

Rainfrog gives users a reusable “Creative OS”—asset libraries and a canvas workflow engine—so a look, once built, can be reused indefinitely:

- Asset libraries. Upload products, characters, styles, and environments once; Rainfrog analyzes each asset and keeps it ready to drop into any workflow.
- Node-based workflows.Connect asset nodes to generator nodes on a canvas to describe a shot, campaign, or scene —no prompt writing required.
- AI image & video generation. Generate on-model fashion shots, product stills, UGC-style social content, and video from the assembled workflow, in normal or pro quality.
- Templates & public gallery. Start from ready-made presets (Fashion Shooting, Product Still Life, UGC Content) or clone a workflow from the public gallery and swap in your own assets.
- Projects. Every canvas is an auto-saved project with undo/redo, so workflows can be revisited, remixed, and scaled across campaigns.

Rainfrog is offered as a subscription product with monthly credits for AI generation. Anyone can [sign up for free](https://app.rainfrog.ai/signup) to explore the studio; paid plans add credits, storage, and production features. See [pricing](https://www.rainfrog.ai/pricing) for current plans.

## Who it serves

Rainfrog is built for art directors, not engineers—for people who think in shots, styles, and campaigns rather than prompts and pipelines:

- Art directors who want to direct visual workflows on a canvas instead of writing prompts by hand.
- Creative directors who need a consistent brand look across every image and video, driven by reusable style and environment libraries.
- Marketing and brand teams who need to scale campaign visuals without reshooting for every format or channel.
- Fashion and e-commerce teams producing on-model shots, product stills, and outfit workflows at scale.
- Social and UGC teams creating creator-style, feed-native visuals from presets.

Rainfrog is intentionally not aimed at developers building custom AI pipelines or prompt-engineering workflows—its canvas and libraries exist so creative direction, not engineering, drives the output.

## How it works

1. Upload products, characters, styles, and environments to your libraries once.
2. Open the studio canvas, drag a template or start from scratch, and connect asset nodes to a generator node.
3. Rainfrog traverses the node graph and assembles the prompt automatically in the backend, with taste already embedded.
4. Click Generate to produce campaign-ready images or video.
5. Iterate: swap assets, chain nodes, and reuse the workflow across new campaigns.
