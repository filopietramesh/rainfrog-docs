A workflow is a saved node graph — your configured canvas — that you can run repeatedly or clone as a starting point. Every project in Rainfrog holds a workflow. Once you have a graph that produces the output you want, reuse it for new campaigns by swapping assets instead of rebuilding from scratch.

## What a workflow contains

A workflow stores everything on your canvas:

- The **nodes** you placed (products, characters, styles, environments, text/image/video inputs, generators, notes)
- The **connections** between those nodes
- **Asset selections** inside each node
- **Generator settings** — model choice, aspect ratio, resolution, and related options

All of this auto-saves inside the project. When you reopen a project, the canvas restores as you left it.

## Starting from a template

Rainfrog includes three built-in templates designed for common campaign types. Add them from the **studio floating dock**, not from the Workflows tab.

| Template | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Fashion Shooting** | Apparel and accessories on a character |
| **Product Still Life** | Packshot and object-focused campaigns |
| **UGC Content** | Casual, creator-style social visuals |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a project canvas">
    Create or open a project in [app.rainfrog.ai](https://app.rainfrog.ai).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a template from the dock">
    Open the floating dock and choose Fashion Shooting, Product Still Life, or UGC Content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap in your assets">
    Replace placeholders with your product, character, style, or environment and click **Generate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The public gallery

The public gallery is a curated set of workflows you can clone into your account. Browse it in the **Workflows** tab or at [rainfrog.ai/workflows](https://www.rainfrog.ai/workflows).

Examples of looks you may see include soft studio still lifes, beauty sets, streetwear editorials, and lifestyle interiors. The exact list changes as new workflows are published.

To clone a gallery workflow, open it and click **Clone**. Rainfrog copies the node graph into a new project. Swap in your assets and generate.

## Remix and reuse

Once you have a working graph:

- Duplicate a **project** when you want a parallel creative direction
- Swap one asset at a time to explore variations without losing consistency
- Use **Save to Gallery** from the studio when you want to snapshot a configuration for later

Project limits still apply (Hobby 6 / Pro 25 / Ultra unlimited). See [Plans and Credits](/plans-and-credits).
