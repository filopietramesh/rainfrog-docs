A workflow is a saved node graph — your fully configured canvas — that you can run repeatedly, share with the community, or clone from someone else. Every project in Rainfrog is a workflow. Once you've built a workflow that produces the output you want, you can reuse it for new campaigns simply by swapping in different assets, without rebuilding anything from scratch.

## What a workflow contains

A workflow stores everything on your canvas:

- The **nodes** you've placed: product, character, style, environment, and generator nodes
- The **connections** (wires) between those nodes
- Any **asset selections** made within each node — which product image, which character, which style
- The **generator settings** — image vs. video, quality mode, and any run configuration

All of this is saved automatically inside the project. When you reopen a project, the canvas restores exactly as you left it, ready to generate again.

## Starting from a template

Rainfrog includes three built-in workflow templates designed for the most common campaign types. Templates give you a fully wired canvas to start from — no setup required.

| Template | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Fashion Shooting** | Apparel and accessories campaigns featuring a model character |
| **Product Still Life** | Packshot and object-focused campaigns without a character |
| **UGC Content** | Casual, creator-style content with an authentic, handheld aesthetic |

To load a template:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Workflows tab">
    In the [Rainfrog studio](https://app.rainfrog.ai), click the **Workflows** tab in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse templates">
    Click **Browse Templates** to see all available presets. Select the template that matches your campaign type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open in a new project">
    Click **Use Template**. Rainfrog opens the template as a new project with all nodes pre-wired and ready to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap in your assets">
    Replace the placeholder nodes with your own product, character, style, or environment assets and click **Generate**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Building your own workflow from scratch

If you want full creative control from the beginning, start with a blank canvas.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new project">
    Click **New Project** in the studio. An empty canvas opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add asset nodes">
    Drag a **Product**, **Character**, **Style**, and/or **Environment** node onto the canvas from the node panel. You don't need all four — use what your campaign requires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure each node">
    Click each node to select your asset: choose a product from your library, pick a stock character or upload a reference, select a style, and set your environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a generator node">
    Drag a **Generator** node onto the canvas and connect your asset nodes to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Choose your output type (image or video) and quality mode, then click **Generate**. Your workflow saves automatically as you work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reusing and remixing

Once you have a workflow that produces results you like, you have two paths for reusing it:

- **Duplicate the project** to preserve the original workflow while experimenting with a variation. Duplication copies the entire canvas into a new project — the original remains untouched.
- **Swap an asset node** directly in the existing project to repurpose the workflow for a new campaign. For example, replace the product node with a new garment to instantly adapt a fashion shoot workflow for a different SKU, or replace the character node to feature a different model without changing anything else.

Both approaches let you build a library of proven workflows that you can remix quickly for new clients, new seasons, or new product lines — without starting from scratch each time.

<Info>
  Each Rainfrog account project is its own saved workflow. You can have up to 6 (Hobby), 25 (Pro), or unlimited (Ultra) projects.
</Info>
