When you click **Generate**, Rainfrog traverses your node graph, resolves the output of every connected asset node, and assembles the AI generation instructions automatically. Within seconds, it begins producing campaign-ready images or video — no prompt writing, no manual parameter tuning. The output is designed to look like it came from a coordinated creative shoot, not a series of one-off generations.

## Image generation

The generator node produces a **campaign set** — multiple images in a single run — not a single image. Every image in the set shares the same style, character, product, and environment anchors, so they look cohesive and intentional when used together.

This is the core difference between Rainfrog and a standard image generator: the output is campaign-ready by design. You get a collection of visuals that work together as a unified campaign, ready for use across different formats, placements, and channels.

<Note>
  Rainfrog generates a campaign *set* — multiple images per run — not a single image. This is by design: the output is meant to be used together as a unified visual campaign.
</Note>

## Normal vs. Pro quality

Every image generation run lets you choose between two quality modes.

| Mode | Best for | Speed | Credit cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal** | Iteration, drafts, creative exploration | Faster | Lower |
| **Pro** | Final campaign output, client delivery | Priority queue on paid plans | Higher |

Normal quality is faster and uses fewer credits, making it ideal for working through multiple creative directions quickly. Pro quality produces higher-resolution output with greater detail and better product fidelity — use it when you're ready to deliver.

<Tip>
  Use Normal quality for fast iteration while refining your workflow. Switch to Pro quality for the final campaign output.
</Tip>

## Video generation

The same workflow that generates a campaign image set can also produce short-form campaign video clips. To generate video, connect your asset nodes to a **video generator node** instead of (or in addition to) the image generator node.

The same asset consistency that applies to images carries over to video: the character, product, style, and environment anchors remain locked across every frame, so your video output matches the visual world of your image campaign.

Video generation is available alongside image generation within the same project — you don't need to rebuild your workflow to produce both formats.

## Credits

Each generation run costs credits. The exact credit cost depends on two factors:

- **Output type** — image runs cost fewer credits than video runs
- **Quality mode** — Normal runs cost fewer credits than Pro runs

Your current credit balance is always visible in the studio header. Credits refresh each month on your billing date and **roll over** — unused credits don't expire at the end of the month.

If you run low on credits before your next billing date, you can purchase a one-time top-up at any time from **Settings → Manage Plan** without changing your subscription tier.

## Generation time

Rainfrog processes your generation in the background so you can keep working while it runs.

| Output type | Typical time |
|---|---|
| Image campaign set (Normal) | Under 95 seconds |
| Image campaign set (Pro) | Priority queue on Pro and Ultra plans |
| Video | Varies by clip length |

Pro and Ultra plan subscribers receive priority queue access for Pro quality generations, which reduces wait times during peak usage.

## Reviewing and iterating

After a generation run completes, the output appears directly in your project canvas. From there:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the campaign set">
    Browse all images (or the video clip) produced in the run. Compare them to evaluate composition, product placement, character consistency, and overall aesthetic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify what to change">
    Decide which element to adjust — the environment, the style, the character pose, or the product framing. Each of these maps to a specific node on your canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap the relevant node">
    Click the node you want to change and select a different asset, or replace the node entirely. You don't need to touch any other part of the workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate again">
    Click **Generate** to produce a fresh campaign set with the updated configuration. Each run is independent — your previous outputs remain visible in the project history so you can compare runs side by side.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Iterating on a Rainfrog workflow is fast because changing one node only changes one variable. You keep everything else — the character, the style, the product — consistent while you dial in the element you want to refine.
