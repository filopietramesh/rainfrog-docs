Asset libraries are the building blocks of every Rainfrog workflow. Each asset type — product, character, style, and environment — maps directly to a node on the canvas. You upload or configure an asset once, and it becomes available to drop into any workflow across your account. There's no need to re-upload the same product image for every new campaign.

## Products

A product asset is the item you want to feature in your campaign. Rainfrog supports any physical product you can photograph: garments, accessories, bags, furniture, packshots, and lifestyle product images.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your product image">
    Navigate to the **Assets** tab in the [Rainfrog studio](https://app.rainfrog.ai) and select **Products**. Click **Upload** and choose your image file. High-resolution images with a clean background yield the best results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Rainfrog analyze the asset">
    After upload, Rainfrog automatically analyzes your product — identifying its shape, texture, color, and key visual properties. This analysis is what lets the generator node place the product accurately in any scene.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop it into a workflow">
    Once analysis is complete, your product appears in your library. Drag it onto the canvas as a **Product node** and connect it to a generator node. You can swap the product in any existing workflow without rebuilding the rest of the graph.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Product limits by plan:**

| Plan | Product assets |
|---|---|
| Hobby | 25 |
| Pro | 100 |
| Ultra | Unlimited |

<Tip>
  Upload your hero product images at the start of a season. You can reuse the same product node across 10 different workflows without re-uploading.
</Tip>

## Characters

A character asset defines the person or model who appears in your campaign. You have two options: pick a stock character from Rainfrog's built-in library, or upload your own reference image to create a custom character.

- **Stock characters** are professionally sourced models already in Rainfrog's library. Browse and select directly from the Character node panel — no upload required.
- **Custom characters** let you upload a reference photo of a real person or model. Rainfrog uses reference-based conditioning (not a text description) to maintain that character's face, proportions, and body language consistently across every image in your campaign.

Character consistency is especially valuable for brand campaigns where you need the same talent to appear across multiple scenes and settings without a live shoot.

**Character limits by plan:**

| Plan | Character assets |
|---|---|
| Hobby | 10 |
| Pro | 40 |
| Ultra | Unlimited |

## Styles

A style asset is a visual preset that encodes three properties: **color palette**, **lighting schema**, and **mood**. Every image generated from a workflow that includes a style node inherits all three properties simultaneously — no text description needed.

You have two ways to define a style:

- **Built-in styles** are pre-configured by Rainfrog's creative team. Browse and select them directly from the Style node panel.
- **Custom styles** let you upload one or more reference images that represent the visual treatment you want. Rainfrog extracts the aesthetic from your references and applies it to every image in the run.

Because the style node drives the look of the entire campaign set, swapping one style node is all it takes to completely change the visual direction of a workflow.

## Environments

An environment asset defines where your campaign takes place — the background, setting, and world that surrounds your product and character.

- **Built-in environments** cover common campaign scenarios: studio setups, outdoor locations, interior spaces, and abstract backgrounds.
- **Custom environments** accept a reference image upload. Rainfrog uses the reference to anchor the composition's spatial feel, depth, and context.

The environment node works alongside the style node: the style governs the *look* (color, light, mood), while the environment governs the *place* (location, setting, spatial context).

## Managing your library

All four asset types live in the **Assets** tab inside the [Rainfrog studio](https://app.rainfrog.ai). Your library is shared across every project in your account — an asset you upload in one project is immediately available in all others.

Key things to keep in mind:

- **Search and filter** your library by asset type or name to find assets quickly as your library grows.
- **Renaming** an asset updates its name everywhere it appears in the studio.
- **Deleting** an asset is permanent and affects every workflow that references it.

<Warning>
  Deleting an asset from your library removes it from every workflow that references it. Regenerating those workflows will not include the deleted asset.
</Warning>
