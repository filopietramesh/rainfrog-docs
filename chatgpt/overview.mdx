Rainfrog connects to ChatGPT through a custom MCP connector. You stay in ChatGPT for briefs and iteration, while projects, assets, and generations live in your Rainfrog account at [app.rainfrog.ai](https://app.rainfrog.ai).

<Note>
  This Guide focuses on ChatGPT. The same connector URL also works in other MCP clients such as Claude, Cursor, and Grok — follow each product’s connector settings with the URL from [Connect](/chatgpt/connect).
</Note>

## What you can do

After you connect and sign in with OAuth, you can ask ChatGPT to work with Rainfrog on your behalf:

- **Campaigns and presets** — start fashion, product, lookbook, and related campaign runs
- **Characters and products** — create and reuse library assets
- **Projects** — list, open, and edit studio projects from chat
- **Generate and export** — run generations and get results you can open or download in Rainfrog

You still use the full node canvas in the web studio when you want hands-on graph editing. ChatGPT is another way into the same workspace.

## What you need

1. A **ChatGPT** account that allows **custom MCP** connectors (availability depends on your ChatGPT plan and workspace settings)
2. A **Rainfrog** account with an active paid plan and credits for generations — same [plans and credits](/plans-and-credits) as the studio
3. No API keys. You authorize Rainfrog once with OAuth when ChatGPT connects

## ChatGPT vs studio

| Use ChatGPT when… | Use the studio when… |
| --- | --- |
| You want to brief campaigns in conversation | You want to wire nodes on the canvas |
| You need quick asset or project actions from chat | You need precise layout and multi-node graphs |
| You’re iterating with Rainfrog from another AI client | You’re art-directing a full workflow visually |

Credits, libraries, and projects are shared. What you create in ChatGPT shows up in [app.rainfrog.ai](https://app.rainfrog.ai).
