Connect Rainfrog once with a custom MCP URL. ChatGPT opens Rainfrog’s sign-in and consent screen, then you can use Rainfrog from a new chat.

<Tip>
  Rainfrog is not listed in the ChatGPT directory yet. Use the **custom MCP** steps below — do not look for a directory install.
</Tip>

## Before you start

- Sign up or sign in at [app.rainfrog.ai](https://app.rainfrog.ai)
- Confirm you have a paid plan with credits if you plan to generate ([Plans & Credits](/plans-and-credits))
- Use ChatGPT on the web with a plan or workspace that allows custom MCP connectors

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open custom MCP settings in ChatGPT">
    In ChatGPT, open your connector or plugin settings and choose to **add a custom MCP server** (wording may vary slightly by ChatGPT version).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste the Rainfrog MCP URL">
    Enter this URL exactly:

    ```text
    https://mcp.rainfrog.ai/mcp
    ```

    Save or continue. ChatGPT starts the Rainfrog authorization flow.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Allow access on Rainfrog">
    Sign in to Rainfrog if prompted. On the consent screen, review what ChatGPT can access, then click **Allow**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm and start a chat">
    Return to ChatGPT and confirm the connector is connected. Open a **new chat** and ask Rainfrog to help — for example, list your projects or start a campaign from a preset.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You never paste API keys or secrets into chat. Connection uses OAuth only.
</Note>

## Disconnect or limit access

- **Revoke the app:** In Rainfrog, open the dashboard → **Settings** → **Connected apps**, then remove ChatGPT (or the connector you authorized).
- **Limit agent actions:** In Rainfrog, open agent permission settings and set **Deny** for action families you do not want the connector to perform (read, add, edit, delete, or generate).

After you revoke, reconnect anytime by repeating the steps above.

## Other MCP clients

The same URL works in other MCP clients. Add `https://mcp.rainfrog.ai/mcp` as a connector, complete OAuth when prompted, and use your Rainfrog account.
