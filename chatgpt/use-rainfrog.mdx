Once Rainfrog is connected, ask ChatGPT in plain language. Rainfrog uses your account libraries, projects, and credits — the same ones you see in the web studio.

<Tip>
  Generation uses your Rainfrog credits. Cost follows the same rules as the studio. Check **Manage Plan** in [app.rainfrog.ai](https://app.rainfrog.ai) if you need more credits.
</Tip>

## Example tasks

Try prompts like these (adjust names and details to your brand):

- **Start a campaign** — “Start a fashion editorial campaign with my summer dress product and Mira character.”
- **Product still life** — “Run a product hero preset for my sneakers on a marble surface.”
- **Create assets** — “Create a character from these reference photos, then start a lookbook.”
- **Open a project** — “List my Rainfrog projects and open the Spring Campaign one.”
- **Edit and regenerate** — “Regenerate shot 2 of the last campaign and give me a download link.”
- **Export** — “When the run finishes, open the project in Rainfrog and share the download link.”

ChatGPT may ask you to confirm actions that change your workspace or spend credits. Confirm when the request matches what you want.

## Where results live

- **Projects and assets** appear in [app.rainfrog.ai](https://app.rainfrog.ai) under **Projects** and **Assets**
- **Generations** use your plan credits the same way studio generators do
- **Downloads and contact sheets** open in Rainfrog or via links ChatGPT returns when a run finishes

You can continue the same project on the studio canvas anytime — swap nodes, add a **Video Generator**, or refine the graph by hand.

<Note>
  Billing and checkout stay in Rainfrog. ChatGPT can explain your plan and credits, but you buy upgrades and top-ups from **Manage Plan** in the app.
</Note>

## Tips for better runs

- Name products and characters clearly so ChatGPT can pick the right library items
- Reuse locked characters and products across shots for brand consistency ([Brand Consistency](/guides/brand-consistency))
- Open the project URL in the studio when you want precise canvas control ([Node Canvas](/concepts/node-canvas))
