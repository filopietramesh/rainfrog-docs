Rainfrog's built-in templates and public gallery give you a fast path to campaign-quality workflows. Instead of building a node graph from scratch, you start from a pre-configured setup and swap in your own assets. This is the fastest way to go from zero to a running campaign if you're new to Rainfrog or exploring a new visual direction.

## Built-in templates

Three templates are available on all plans. Each one comes with a pre-configured node graph optimized for a specific type of campaign output.

**Fashion Shooting**
Configured for on-model apparel and fashion imagery. The node graph is optimized for garment rendering — keeping fabric texture, fit, and color accurate on a character in a styled environment. Use this for lookbooks, seasonal campaigns, and editorial fashion content.

**Product Still Life**
Configured for clean, controlled product photography. Ideal for packshots, beauty products, accessories, and any campaign where the product itself is the hero. The graph is set up to anchor the product tightly and minimize environmental distraction.

**UGC Content**
Configured for creator-style, feed-native social visuals. The aesthetic is casual and lifestyle-forward — designed to produce content that feels organic on social feeds rather than polished and editorial.

To load any template, go to the **Workflows** tab and click the template you want. It opens as a new project with the node graph already configured and ready for your assets.

## The public gallery

The public gallery is a collection of community-created workflows that show the full range of what Rainfrog can produce. Browse it to find a visual direction that matches your campaign brief before building anything yourself.

Current gallery workflows include: White Flower, Interior Editorial, Set Selfie, Beauty Set, Chanel Still Life, Street Rockstar, Golden Room, Into the Ice, Fire Jacket, Poker Dinner, City Horse, and Blue Suit — among others added by the community regularly.

Browse the full gallery at [https://www.rainfrog.ai/workflows](https://www.rainfrog.ai/workflows).

## Cloning a gallery workflow

Click **Clone** on any gallery workflow you want to use. It opens as a new project in your account with the full node graph intact — all the connections, the generator settings, and the structure that produced the original output are preserved.

The original workflow's assets are replaced with placeholders when you clone. You'll see the node types and connections, but the asset slots are empty and waiting for your own content.

<Info>
  Cloning a gallery workflow does not affect the original. Your cloned copy is fully yours to modify and regenerate.
</Info>

## Customizing after cloning

Once you've cloned a workflow, follow these steps to make it your own:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the cloned project">
    Find the cloned project in your **Projects** tab and open it. The canvas loads with the full node graph from the original workflow.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify each asset node">
    Click each asset node on the canvas to see what type it is — Product, Character, Style, or Environment. The node panel shows the asset type and the empty slot where the original asset was.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Replace with your own assets">
    For each asset node, select an asset from your library or upload a new one. Replace the Product node with your product image, the Character node with your character reference, the Style node with your brand's visual preset, and the Environment node with your preferred setting.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Leave the generator node and connections intact">
    Don't disconnect or reconfigure the Generator node or the wires between nodes. The connection structure from the original workflow is what makes it produce strong output — keep it in place.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Generate">
    Click **Generate**. Rainfrog runs the graph with your assets in place and produces a campaign set using the same node structure as the original workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Browse the public gallery at [https://www.rainfrog.ai/workflows](https://www.rainfrog.ai/workflows) to find a workflow whose visual aesthetic matches your campaign vision before cloning.
</Tip>
