Rainfrog's studio templates and public gallery give you a fast path to campaign-quality workflows. Start from a pre-configured graph, then swap in your own assets.

## Studio templates

Three templates are available on all plans. Add them from the **floating dock** inside a project — not from the Workflows tab.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/template-fashion-shooting.jpg" alt="Fashion Shooting template preview" />
</Frame>

**Fashion Shooting**
Configured for on-model apparel and fashion imagery. Optimized for garment rendering on a character in a styled environment. Use it for lookbooks, seasonal campaigns, and editorial fashion.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/template-product-still-life.jpg" alt="Product Still Life template preview" />
</Frame>

**Product Still Life**
Configured for clean product photography. Ideal for packshots, beauty, accessories, and product-hero campaigns.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/template-ugc-content.jpg" alt="UGC Content template preview" />
</Frame>

**UGC Content**
Configured for creator-style, feed-native social visuals — casual and lifestyle-forward.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a project">
    Create or open a project so the studio canvas is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a template from the dock">
    Open the floating dock and choose Fashion Shooting, Product Still Life, or UGC Content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace assets">
    Swap placeholder products, characters, styles, and environments for your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate** on the Image Generator (or other generator in the template).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The public gallery

The public gallery shows curated workflows you can clone. Browse it in the **Workflows** tab inside the app, or on the marketing site at [https://www.rainfrog.ai/workflows](https://www.rainfrog.ai/workflows).

Gallery entries change over time. Expect a mix of still lifes, beauty, streetwear, and lifestyle looks — use the previews to find a tone that matches your brief.

## Cloning a gallery workflow

Click **Clone** on any gallery workflow you want. It opens as a new project with the node graph structure preserved so you can swap assets and regenerate.

<Info>
  Cloning does not change the original gallery entry. Your copy is yours to edit.
</Info>

## Customizing after cloning

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the cloned project">
    Find it under **Projects** and open the canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify each asset node">
    Click Product, Character, Style, and Environment nodes to see what to replace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace with your assets">
    Select items from your libraries (or built-ins). Prefer keeping generator connections intact when you only need asset swaps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate**. Rainfrog runs the graph with your assets in the cloned structure.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Browse [rainfrog.ai/workflows](https://www.rainfrog.ai/workflows) to match aesthetic before you clone, then swap product and character first.
</Tip>
