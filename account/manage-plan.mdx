The **Manage Plan** tab is your hub for subscription and credits. From here you can see your plan and balance, change tiers, buy top-ups, open billing, pause, or cancel — without leaving the app.

## Viewing your plan and credits

Open **Manage Plan** in [app.rainfrog.ai](https://app.rainfrog.ai) to see:

- Your **current plan** (Hobby, Pro, or Ultra)
- Your **billing cycle** (monthly or annual)
- Your **credit usage** — available and used
- Status such as active, pausing, or cancelling at period end

Check this tab before a large campaign batch so you know how many credits you have left.

## Changing your plan

### Upgrading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Plan">
    Select **Manage Plan** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Upgrade">
    Use the upgrade control (for example **Upgrade plan** or **Upgrade to Hobby**) to open pricing and pick a higher tier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm checkout">
    Complete checkout. On a successful upgrade, the higher credit allotment and limits apply for your new plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Downgrading

Move to a lower tier from pricing / plan selection when available. Lower limits take effect according to your billing change. Projects and assets above the new limits are **not deleted**, but you may need to stay within the new caps to create more.

<Warning>
  Downgrading can reduce project and asset limits. Items above the new caps are not deleted automatically.
</Warning>

## Adding credits (top-ups)

If monthly credits run low, buy a one-time pack without changing your plan.

- Use **Top up credits** or **Buy 1,000 credits** on Manage Plan
- Top-up credits stay available until you use them
- They stack with your monthly allotment

## Manage billing

Rainfrog processes payments through **Stripe**. Use **Manage billing** on the Manage Plan page to open the customer portal for invoices, payment methods, and related billing details.

<Info>
  For billing issues, contact [support@rainfrog.ai](mailto:support@rainfrog.ai).
</Info>

## Pause subscription

If you need a break, you can **pause** from the cancel flow. While paused:

- You can browse projects and libraries
- Generation is unavailable until you resume
- Use **Resume subscription** on Manage Plan when you are ready to continue

## Cancel subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Plan">
    Select **Manage Plan** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Cancel subscription">
    Follow the confirmation prompts. You may also see pause as an alternative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access until the period ends">
    Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current billing period**. You keep plan access until that date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Rainfrog does not offer prorated refunds for unused subscription time or credits. Cancel before your next renewal if you want to avoid the next charge.
</Warning>

<Note>
  After a plan ends, your projects and assets remain in the account, but generation stays unavailable until you subscribe again.
</Note>

## Annual vs. monthly billing

| | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| **Billed** | Each month | Once per year |
| **Discount** | — | 20% off |
| **Credits** | Added monthly | Added monthly throughout the year |
| **Cancel** | End of current period | End of current period |

Both cycles deliver credits on a monthly cadence. Annual billing pre-pays the year at a discount. Switch between monthly and annual from pricing when you change plans.
