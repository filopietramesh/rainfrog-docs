The **Manage Plan** tab in the Rainfrog studio is your central hub for everything subscription-related. From here, you can view your current plan and credit balance, change your subscription tier, purchase one-time credit top-ups, and access your billing history — all without leaving the app.

## Viewing your plan and credits

Open the **Manage Plan** tab inside [app.rainfrog.ai](https://app.rainfrog.ai) to see a snapshot of your account at a glance. The tab displays:

- Your **current plan name** (Hobby, Pro, or Ultra)
- Your **billing cycle** (monthly or annual)
- Your **credit balance** — credits used and credits remaining for the current period
- Your **next renewal date**

Check this tab whenever you need to gauge how many credits you have left before kicking off a large campaign.

## Changing your plan

You can upgrade or downgrade your subscription at any time from the Manage Plan tab.

### Upgrading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Plan">
    Navigate to the **Manage Plan** tab in the Rainfrog studio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a new plan">
    Click **Upgrade** and choose the plan that fits your needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the change">
    Review the pricing summary and confirm. On monthly billing, the upgrade takes effect immediately and you gain access to the higher credit allotment and limits right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Downgrading

To move to a lower-tier plan, select the plan you want in the Manage Plan tab and confirm the downgrade. The change takes effect at the **end of your current billing period** — you keep your existing plan's features until then.

<Warning>
  Downgrading may reduce your project and asset limits. Projects and assets above the new plan's limits are **not deleted**, but they become inaccessible until you upgrade again.
</Warning>

## Adding credits (top-ups)

If your monthly credit allotment runs low mid-campaign, you can purchase a one-time credit top-up pack without changing your plan.

- Top-up packs are available directly from the **Manage Plan** tab.
- Top-up credits **never expire** — they stay in your account until you use them.
- Top-up credits stack with your monthly allotment, so they're ideal for high-volume production sprints.

<Tip>
  Keep a top-up pack on hand before launching a large campaign batch so generation never stalls while you wait for your monthly credits to renew.
</Tip>

## Billing and invoices

Rainfrog processes all payments securely through **Stripe**. After each charge, an invoice is automatically emailed to the address on your Rainfrog account. You can also access and download past invoices at any time from the **Billing** section of the Manage Plan tab.

<Info>
  For billing issues or disputes, contact [support@rainfrog.ai](mailto:support@rainfrog.ai).
</Info>

## Annual vs. monthly billing

| | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| **Billed** | Each month | Once per year |
| **Discount** | — | 20% off |
| **Credits** | Added monthly | Added monthly on renewal date |
| **Flexibility** | Cancel anytime | Locked for the year |

Both billing cycles deliver credits on the same monthly cadence — annual billing simply pre-pays for the year at a discounted rate. To switch from monthly to annual, go to the Manage Plan tab and select the annual option. The switch takes effect immediately, and a prorated credit may apply for the remainder of your current month.

## Canceling your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Plan">
    Navigate to the **Manage Plan** tab in the Rainfrog studio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Cancel Plan">
    Select **Cancel Plan** and follow the confirmation prompts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retain access until period ends">
    Your cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current billing period**. You keep full access to generation and all plan features until that date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Rainfrog does not offer prorated refunds for unused subscription time or credits. Cancel before your next renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.
</Warning>

<Note>
  If you cancel and your plan expires, your projects and assets remain in your account but generation is paused until you resubscribe.
</Note>
