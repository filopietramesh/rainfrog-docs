Below are answers to the most common questions about Rainfrog, covering everything from your first generation run to credits, billing, asset management, and getting help from the team. If you don't find what you're looking for here, reach out to [support@rainfrog.ai](mailto:support@rainfrog.ai).

## Getting Started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a paid plan to use Rainfrog?">
    No — a free account lets you explore the studio, browse workflows, and set up your asset library without subscribing. However, generating images and videos with the full credit allotment requires an active paid plan (Hobby, Pro, or Ultra).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What browsers does Rainfrog support?">
    Rainfrog works best in modern desktop browsers: **Chrome**, **Firefox**, **Safari**, and **Edge** (latest versions recommended). Mobile browsers are not optimized for the node canvas, so use a desktop or laptop for the best experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to know how to write AI prompts?">
    No. Rainfrog is designed so you never write a prompt manually. You direct the AI by connecting asset nodes — products, characters, environments — through the workflow canvas. The studio translates your visual inputs into generation instructions automatically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Generation & Output

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does my generation not look like my reference images?">
    A few things to check:

    - Make sure your asset nodes are **properly connected** to the generator node in the canvas.
    - Try uploading a **higher-quality** product or character reference image — sharper, well-lit photos produce more faithful output.
    - Switch to **Pro quality** mode in the generator node for more detail-accurate results (note: Pro quality costs more credits per run).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many images are produced per generation run?">
    Rainfrog generates a campaign *set* — multiple images — per run. The exact number of images in the set depends on your workflow configuration. Check the generator node settings to see the output count before you generate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I generate a single image instead of a set?">
    Rainfrog is purpose-built for campaign-set generation, so producing a single image in isolation is not the primary use case. For single-image output, adjust your workflow configuration, but keep in mind the platform is optimized for multi-asset campaign production.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is generation taking a long time?">
    Generation time varies based on server load and your plan tier:

    - **Pro and Ultra** plans have access to the **priority generation queue**, which reduces wait times significantly.
    - A typical image set on the priority queue completes in **under 95 seconds**.
    - Video generation takes longer than image generation regardless of plan.

    If generation is consistently slow, consider upgrading to Pro or Ultra for priority access.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Rainfrog-generated images commercially?">
    Yes. Images generated using your account and your uploaded assets are yours to use commercially. Review the [Terms of Service](https://app.rainfrog.ai) for full licensing details and any applicable restrictions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Credits & Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many credits does a generation cost?">
    Credit cost depends on two factors:

    - **Quality mode** — Normal quality costs fewer credits per run; Pro quality costs more.
    - **Output type** — Video generation costs significantly more credits than image generation.

    The exact credit cost for your current settings is displayed in the generator node **before** you start a run, so you always know what you're spending.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My credits ran out. What should I do?">
    You have two options:

    1. **Purchase a one-time top-up** from the **Manage Plan** tab. Top-up credits never expire and are added to your balance immediately.
    2. **Upgrade to a higher plan** (Hobby → Pro → Ultra) for a larger monthly credit allotment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do credits expire?">
    It depends on the credit type:

    - **Monthly subscription credits** roll over each month when your subscription renews — they accumulate rather than resetting to zero.
    - **One-time top-up credits** never expire and remain in your account until you use them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I was charged but can't access my plan features.">
    Contact [support@rainfrog.ai](mailto:support@rainfrog.ai) with your account email address and a description of the issue. Billing-related access problems are typically resolved within **one business day**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Assets & Storage

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What image formats can I upload for assets?">
    **JPG and PNG** are the recommended formats for all asset types (products, characters, environments). Always upload the highest-quality version of your reference image available — resolution and clarity directly affect generation fidelity.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I deleted an asset by mistake. Can I recover it?">
    No. Asset deletion in Rainfrog is **permanent** and cannot be undone. If you need the asset again, re-upload the original file from your local storage.

    <Warning>
      Always keep local copies of your reference images before deleting them from Rainfrog.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm hitting my storage limit. What can I do?">
    You have two options:

    - **Free up space** by deleting unused or outdated assets from the **Assets** tab.
    - **Upgrade your plan** — Hobby includes 5 GB, Pro includes 10 GB, and Ultra includes 20 GB of storage.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do my assets work across all my projects?">
    Yes. Every asset you upload to your library is available across **all projects** in your account. You don't need to re-upload the same product or character for each new project.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account & Support

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    - **Product help and billing:** [support@rainfrog.ai](mailto:support@rainfrog.ai)
    - **Partnerships and press:** [info@rainfrog.ai](mailto:info@rainfrog.ai)

    The support team aims to respond to all inquiries within one business day.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share my workflows with teammates?">
    Team and collaboration features depend on your current plan. For multi-seat or agency needs, contact [info@rainfrog.ai](mailto:info@rainfrog.ai) to discuss options tailored to your team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Send a deletion request to [support@rainfrog.ai](mailto:support@rainfrog.ai) from your account email address.

    <Warning>
      Account deletion is **permanent and irreversible**. It removes all of your projects, assets, and generated content. Make sure to download anything you want to keep before submitting a deletion request.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
