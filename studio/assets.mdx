The Assets tab is where you build your creative library. Upload your products, define your characters, pin down your visual styles, and set your environments once — then pull any of them into any workflow without re-uploading. Your entire asset library is available across all your projects.

<Info>
  Assets are shared across all your projects. Update an asset once and every workflow that references it will use the updated version on next generation.
</Info>

## Products

A product is a physical item you want to feature in generated imagery — a garment, packshot, bag, or any product photo. Upload it once and Rainfrog analyzes it so it can be accurately reproduced across campaign outputs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Products section">
    Go to the **Assets** tab and select **Products** from the left panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a product image">
    Click the **+** button, then select an image file from your device. JPG and PNG are the recommended formats for best analysis accuracy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Rainfrog analyze it">
    Rainfrog automatically processes the upload, extracting the product's visual characteristics for use in generation. This takes a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and save">
    Give the product a clear name so you can identify it quickly when building node graphs. Click **Save** to add it to your library.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Best practices for product photos:**

- Use a **clean, simple background** — white or light grey works best
- Ensure **good, even lighting** with no harsh shadows obscuring the product
- Make sure the **full product is visible** and not cropped at the edges
- Avoid busy compositions — Rainfrog's analysis works best when the product is the clear subject

<Tip>
  For the best product results, upload a clean, well-lit product photo with a simple background. Rainfrog's analysis works best when the product is clearly visible and unobstructed.
</Tip>

**Product limits by plan:**

| Plan | Product limit |
|---|---|
| Hobby | 25 products |

## Characters

A character is a model or person who appears in your generated campaign imagery. You can use characters from the built-in stock library or upload your own reference image to create a custom character with consistent appearance across generations.

**Using the stock library:**
The stock character library is available on all plans. Browse and select a character directly from the Assets tab — no upload required.

**Uploading a custom character:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Characters">
    In the Assets tab, select **Characters**, then click **+** to upload a new reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a reference image">
    Choose a clear headshot or portrait. The image should have good lighting, and the face should be fully visible and unobstructed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the character">
    Give the character a name. Rainfrog stores the reference and uses it to maintain consistent likeness across every generation that includes this character.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Tips for character uploads:**
- Use a **front-facing portrait** with the face clearly lit
- Avoid sunglasses, hats, or anything that obscures facial features
- A **neutral expression** in the reference image produces the most flexible outputs
- Higher-resolution images yield better consistency

**Character limits by plan:**

| Plan | Character limit |
|---|---|
| Hobby | 10 characters |
| Pro | 40 characters |
| Ultra | Unlimited |

## Styles

A style is a visual preset that defines the look and feel of your generated imagery — including lighting character, color grading, mood, and overall aesthetic. You can choose from Rainfrog's built-in style library or build a custom style from your own reference images.

**Using built-in styles:**
Browse the style library in the Assets tab and click any style to preview it. Select it to make it available as a node in your workflows.

**Creating a custom style:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + in the Styles section">
    Open **Assets → Styles** and click the **+** button to start a new custom style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload 1–3 reference images">
    Choose images that represent your brand's visual identity — a mood board image, a campaign photo, or any visual that captures the aesthetic you want. Using multiple images helps Rainfrog blend and capture the style more accurately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and save your style">
    Give the style a descriptive name (e.g., "Spring Campaign — Warm Tones") and save it. It becomes a reusable preset you can drop into any workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Environments

An environment defines the setting and background in your generated images — from clean studio backdrops to outdoor locations and lifestyle scenes. Rainfrog includes a built-in environment library, and you can upload your own reference images for custom environments.

**Built-in environment library:**
Rainfrog provides a curated set of environments across categories including studio, lifestyle, outdoor, and editorial. Select any built-in environment from the Assets tab to add it to your library and use it in workflows.

**Uploading a custom environment:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Environments in the Assets tab">
    Select **Environments**, then click **+** to upload a reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a reference image">
    Choose an image that represents the setting or background you want. This could be a location photo, an interior, or any scene you want Rainfrog to recreate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and save">
    Name the environment clearly (e.g., "Tokyo Street — Night") and save it. It's now available as an environment node in all your projects.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Environment limits by plan:**

| Plan | Environment limit |
|---|---|
| Hobby | 15 environments |
| Pro | 50 environments |
| Ultra | Unlimited |

## Storage limits

All uploaded assets count toward your plan's storage quota. Generated outputs do not count against storage.

| Plan | Storage |
|---|---|
| Hobby | 5 GB |
| Pro | 10 GB |
| Ultra | 20 GB |

**Tips for managing your storage:**

- **Delete unused assets** — remove products, characters, or environments you no longer use to free up space
- **Compress images before uploading** — most assets don't need to be larger than 2–4 MB; a well-compressed JPG retains quality while saving significant storage
- **Consolidate similar assets** — instead of uploading multiple versions of the same product, upload your best version and rely on style nodes to vary the output

See [Plans and Credits](/plans-and-credits) if you need to upgrade your storage limit.
