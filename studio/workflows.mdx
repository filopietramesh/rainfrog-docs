The Workflows tab is where you discover, start from, and manage workflow configurations. Whether you're reaching for a built-in template to get started quickly, cloning a community workflow from the public gallery, or returning to a configuration you've already built — this is your central hub for workflow management.

## Built-in templates

Rainfrog ships with three workflow templates available on all plans. Each template opens as a new project with a fully pre-configured node graph — asset nodes, generator nodes, and connections already in place. All you need to do is swap in your own assets and generate.

**How to use a template:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Workflows tab">
    Open the **Workflows** tab from the main navigation. Built-in templates appear at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a template">
    Click the template that matches your campaign type. Rainfrog opens it as a new project with the node graph pre-configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace placeholder asset nodes">
    Each template includes placeholder asset nodes. Click any placeholder node and select an asset from your library to replace it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate">
    Once your assets are connected, click **Generate** to run the graph and produce your campaign outputs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Public gallery

The public gallery showcases workflows created and shared by the Rainfrog community. Browse the gallery to find visual styles and campaign setups that match what you're building — then clone any workflow to add it to your account as a new project.

**To clone a gallery workflow:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the gallery">
    Scroll through the public gallery in the Workflows tab. Each workflow shows a preview image and a brief description of its visual style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Clone">
    Click the **Clone** button on the workflow you want. Rainfrog copies the full workflow into your account as a new project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the cloned project">
    The cloned project appears in your Projects tab. Open it to see the full node graph, ready for you to customize.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start with a public gallery workflow that matches your desired visual tone. Swap in your product and character and you have a campaign-ready setup in under two minutes.
</Tip>

## Your saved workflows

Every project you save in Rainfrog also appears in the Workflows tab as a saved configuration. From this view you can:

- **Open** a workflow to return to its canvas
- **Duplicate** a workflow to create a copy for a new campaign variant
- **Delete** a workflow you no longer need

Changes you make to a project are reflected here automatically — your saved workflows always reflect the current state of each project.

## Swapping assets in a cloned workflow

After you clone a gallery workflow, the node graph is fully editable. Swapping in your own assets takes just a few clicks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the cloned project">
    Navigate to the **Projects** tab and open the cloned project. The original node graph loads on the canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an asset node">
    Click any asset node on the canvas — for example, the character node or product node that came with the cloned workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace with your asset">
    In the node's settings panel, click **Replace Asset** and choose an item from your library. The connection to the generator node stays intact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for each asset node">
    Replace as many asset nodes as you need. You can swap a single asset or replace them all — the rest of the workflow configuration remains unchanged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Click **Generate** to run the updated graph with your assets. The workflow's visual style and structure carry over — only the assets change.
  </Step>
</Steps>
