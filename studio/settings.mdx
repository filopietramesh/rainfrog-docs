The Settings tab lets you manage your account preferences, profile information, and notification preferences. It's the right place for anything related to how your account is configured — who you are, how you authenticate, and what communications you receive from Rainfrog.

<Note>
  To manage your subscription, billing, and credits, use the **Manage Plan** tab — not Settings. Settings covers profile and account preferences only.
</Note>

## Profile

Update your display name and email address from **Settings → Profile**.

- **Display name** — the name shown in the Rainfrog studio and on any community-shared workflows. Click the field to edit and save.
- **Email address** — the address associated with your account. If you change your email, Rainfrog will send a verification link to the new address. Your email won't update until you confirm the link.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Profile">
    Navigate to the **Settings** tab and select **Profile** from the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your display name or email">
    Click into the field you want to update and type your changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. If you changed your email address, check your inbox for the verification email and confirm the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password

Change your password from **Settings → Security → Change Password**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Security">
    Select **Security** from the Settings left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change Password">
    Click **Change Password** and enter your current password, followed by your new password. Confirm the new password and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you signed up using a social login (Google, Apple, or similar), Rainfrog does not store a password for your account. Password management for social login accounts is handled entirely through the provider you used to sign up.

## Email notifications

Control which emails Rainfrog sends you from **Settings → Notifications**. You can toggle each category independently.

| Notification type | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Generation complete** | An email when a long-running generation job finishes |
| **Billing alerts** | Notices about upcoming renewals, payment failures, and credit usage |
| **Product updates** | Announcements about new features, templates, and improvements to Rainfrog |

Toggle any category on or off and click **Save preferences**. Changes take effect immediately.

## Account deletion

If you need to permanently delete your Rainfrog account, email **support@rainfrog.ai** with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." The support team will confirm your identity and process the deletion.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. When your account is deleted:

- All projects and canvases are removed
- All uploaded assets (products, characters, styles, environments) are deleted
- All generated outputs stored in your account are removed
- Your subscription is cancelled and no further charges are made

Export any projects or assets you want to keep before submitting a deletion request.

<Info>
  For questions or issues not covered in Settings, email support@rainfrog.ai.
</Info>
