sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"

---

This guide walks you through everything from creating your account to generating your first batch of campaign images. By the end, you'll have a working workflow on the canvas and a set of outputs you can iterate on immediately.

<Tip>
  If you don't have assets ready, every node type has a built-in library you can use — no uploads required to generate your first campaign.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.rainfrog.ai/signup](https://app.rainfrog.ai/signup) and sign up for free. A free account lets you explore the studio, browse templates, and get familiar with the canvas before committing to a plan. When you're ready to run full generation jobs, subscribe to a paid plan from the **Manage Plan** tab — each plan comes with a monthly credit allowance that powers your image and video generation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a new project">
    After you log in, open the **Projects** tab. Click **New Project**, give your project a name, and hit **Create**. The canvas opens automatically and is ready for you to start building. Your project auto-saves as you work, so you won't lose progress if you close the tab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a workflow template">
    On the canvas, click **Browse Templates** — or open the **Workflows** tab in the sidebar — to see the full template library. Pick a preset that matches what you want to create:

    - **Fashion Shooting** — on-model shots with a product and a character
    - **Product Still Life** — styled product placement in an environment
    - **UGC Content** — creator-style social visuals

    Selecting a template drops a pre-wired set of nodes onto your canvas, so you can see how a complete workflow is structured before you change anything.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your assets">
    With the template on the canvas, replace the placeholder nodes with your own assets — or keep the built-in library assets for your first run:

    1. **Product node** — drag a Product node onto the canvas and upload your product image, or select one from the built-in product library.
    2. **Character node** — add a Character node and choose a character reference from your library or the built-in options.
    3. **Style node** — add a Style node to define the visual aesthetic of your output.
    4. **Environment node** — add an Environment node to set the scene or backdrop.

    Connect each asset node to the **Generator node** by dragging from the output port of each asset node to the corresponding input port on the generator. When all inputs are wired up, the generator node shows a ready state.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate">
    Click the **Generate** button. Rainfrog reads your node graph, assembles the AI instructions automatically, and produces a campaign set of images (or video clips, if you're using a video workflow). When generation completes, your outputs appear in the results panel.

    From here, you can:
    - **Swap a node** — disconnect an asset node, connect a different one, and click Generate again to explore a variation
    - **Duplicate the workflow** — copy the entire graph to run parallel creative directions
    - **Download your outputs** — save individual images or the full batch to your device
  </Step>
</Steps>
