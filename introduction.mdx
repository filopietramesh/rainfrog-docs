Rainfrog is a web-based AI studio built for creative and marketing teams who need campaign-quality visuals at scale. Instead of wrestling with text prompts, you build visual workflows on a drag-and-drop canvas — connecting your products, characters, styles, and environments to a generator node. Rainfrog reads the graph and assembles all the AI instructions automatically, so you stay focused on creative direction rather than prompt engineering.

<Note>
  Rainfrog is designed for creative direction, not prompt engineering. You never need to write an AI prompt.
</Note>

## What you can create

Rainfrog produces campaign-ready outputs across a wide range of visual formats:

- **On-model fashion shots** — dress characters in your products and generate editorial-quality imagery
- **Product stills** — place products in styled environments for e-commerce and advertising
- **UGC-style content** — create authentic, creator-style visuals for social campaigns
- **Short video clips** — animate your scenes into shareable video assets
- **Lookbook images** — build cohesive, multi-image lookbooks from a single workflow

## Who Rainfrog is built for

Rainfrog is designed for anyone who directs visual content — not just those who create it technically:

- **Art directors and creative directors** who want to prototype and produce campaigns without depending on a full production pipeline
- **Fashion and e-commerce teams** that need to show products on diverse models and in varied settings, fast
- **Marketing teams** running multi-channel campaigns that demand high volumes of on-brand imagery
- **Solo creators and freelancers** building client work or personal projects with professional-grade output

## How it works

Getting from idea to campaign output takes four steps:

1. **Upload your assets** — add your product images, character references, style references, and environments to your asset libraries once. Every asset you upload is reusable across all your projects.
2. **Build your workflow on the canvas** — open a project, drag asset nodes onto the canvas, and connect them to a generator node. Start from a template or build from scratch.
3. **Generate** — click Generate and Rainfrog runs the node graph, producing a full campaign set of images or video clips in one pass.
4. **Iterate and reuse** — swap any node, adjust connections, and regenerate to explore variations. Your workflow is auto-saved, so every iteration is recoverable.
