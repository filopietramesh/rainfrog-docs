Rainfrog is a web-based AI studio built for creative and marketing teams who need campaign-quality visuals at scale. Instead of wrestling with text prompts, you build visual workflows on a drag-and-drop canvas — connecting your products, characters, styles, and environments to generators. Rainfrog reads the graph and assembles the generation automatically, so you stay focused on creative direction.

<Note>
  Rainfrog is designed for creative direction, not prompt engineering. Most workflows run without writing prompts. An optional Text node is available when you want extra direction.
</Note>

## What you can create

Rainfrog produces campaign-ready outputs across a wide range of visual formats:

- **On-model fashion shots** — dress characters in your products and generate editorial-quality imagery
- **Full-body and outfit views** — use Full Body and Outfit Sheet generators for multi-angle and flat-lay outputs
- **Product stills** — place products in styled environments for e-commerce and advertising
- **UGC-style content** — create authentic, creator-style visuals for social campaigns
- **Short video clips** — turn still workflows into motion with the Video Generator
- **Lookbook imagery** — keep character, product, style, and environment consistent across many iterations

## Who Rainfrog is built for

Rainfrog is designed for anyone who directs visual content — not just those who create it technically:

- **Art directors and creative directors** who want to prototype and produce campaigns without depending on a full production pipeline
- **Fashion and e-commerce teams** that need to show products on diverse talent and in varied settings, fast
- **Marketing teams** running multi-channel campaigns that demand high volumes of on-brand imagery
- **Solo creators and freelancers** building client work or personal projects with professional-grade output

## How it works

Getting from idea to campaign output takes four steps:

1. **Upload your assets** — add products, characters, and environments in the dashboard Assets group. Styles are chosen on the Style node in the studio. Reuse everything across projects.
2. **Build your workflow on the canvas** — open a project, add nodes from the floating dock, and connect them to an Image Generator, Full Body Generator, Outfit Sheet Generator, or Video Generator. Start from a studio template or build from scratch.
3. **Generate** — click **Generate**. Rainfrog runs the graph and produces stills or video on the generator node. Iterate by swapping assets and regenerating.
4. **Reuse** — your project auto-saves. Clone gallery workflows or keep your own graphs as the base for the next campaign.
